Widget home

Where you stand,
at a glance.

Net worth, today's spending, your next payday and the bills coming up, all on one screen. A daily money briefing sums it up in plain English.

  • Net worth across every account
  • Next payday and what's left before it
  • Upcoming bills with real due dates

Swipe pager

A page for every
part of your money.

Home, Spending, Budgets, Plan, Goals and a page for anything else. Flick between them with your finger. The dock stays put.

Dock apps

Tap an app.
Watch it open.

Spending, Budgets, Plan, Goals and Settings sit in the dock. Each one grows out of its icon into the full section, and a swipe up from the bottom sends it home.

Mini apps

Widgets that open
into mini apps.

Tap the Food budget and you get exactly what's left, what that means per day, and every purchase behind it.

Colourways

Twelve looks. Light, dark or auto.

From the classic Vault navy and gold to Harbour, Midnight Gold and Electric Violet, each colourway brings its own type pairing.

Spending

Every dollar, sorted.

This month against last, category by category. Top merchants, a daily spend chart and flags for anything unusual, like a large purchase or a charge that landed twice.

Budgets

Budgets that fill themselves.

Budgets update from your spending automatically. One budget can cover several categories, and the pace marker tells you if you're ahead of where you should be this far into the month.

Plan

See next month before it happens.

A 60-day cash-flow forecast built from your pay schedule, bills and everyday spending. It warns you before chequing dips below your floor, and lists every subscription and loan in one place.

Goals

Save for it. Watch it fill.

Set money aside in virtual accounts inside your real ones, with automatic monthly contributions. Link a goal and Stack tells you exactly how much to save per paycheque.

Make it yours

Press, hold, rearrange.

Every page is yours to design. Add widgets in small, medium or large, drag them into place, and the add-widget gallery only shows what belongs on that page.

Beyond the phone

On your wrist, on your Lock Screen, and everywhere in between.

The native iOS app takes Stack beyond the app, so you can check what's safe to spend without unlocking your phone.
